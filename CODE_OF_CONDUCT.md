# Code of Conduct

FRAME INDEX is a small open-source project. Everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or any project space — is expected to keep it welcoming and useful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; critique work, not people.
- Assume good faith, explain disagreements with evidence, and accept maintainers' decisions gracefully.
- Keep discussion on-topic and free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and spam.
- Respect privacy: never publish anyone's private information (email addresses, credentials, personal details) without their explicit permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information, or encouraging any of the above.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants whose behaviour violates this code, at their discretion and without prior warning for severe cases. Report problems through a GitHub issue marked for maintainer attention, or via the contact links on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled discreetly.

By participating in this project you agree to abide by this code.
